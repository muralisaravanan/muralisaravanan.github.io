Murali Manohar Saravanan

Ph.D. Candidate in Physics, University of Washington

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Physics at the University of Washington, Seattle, working with Marilena Loverde in the Loverde Cosmology Group. My research focuses on how cosmological data — from the CMB, large-scale structure, and beyond — can be used to probe physics beyond the Standard Model, and on how to holistically interpret the resulting constraints.

Before UW, I studied physics at Cornell University, where I worked with Liam McAllister on optimization methods for Calabi–Yau manifolds in string theory, and spent a semester with the CMS collaboration studying neural-network-based particle identification.
